\# PondGuard 🐟



\### Smart Water-Quality Alerts for Small Aquaculture Farms



PondGuard is a simple web-based dashboard designed to help fish farmers monitor important pond water-quality parameters and identify potential risks early.



\## Features



\- Monitor water temperature, dissolved oxygen, pH, total ammonia, and nitrite.

\- Generate alerts when readings cross configured thresholds.

\- Include fish-behavior observations for additional warning signals.

\- View water-quality metrics and risk alerts in a dashboard.

\- Download analysis results as a CSV file.



\## Technology Stack



\- Python

\- Streamlit

\- Pandas

\## Important Disclaimer



PondGuard is currently a prototype that uses configurable, illustrative thresholds and rule-based alerts. Its alerts are not a validated diagnosis of fish disease or a guarantee of water safety. Confirm concerning readings with reliable measurements and appropriate aquaculture expertise before making farm-management decisions.
